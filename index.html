Flight plan

    Welcome to XC GPT ✈

    A planning tool for T-6 cross-country trips: pick a base, see every approved field in range, build a flight plan with local times and weather, and print a crew card.

    1 Pick your base and range

    On the left, choose your departure base and drag the range slider. The map shows fields and airspace within that distance. Switch between the Nav Info List (Vance's approved fields) and P-coded fields, and filter by approach type or turn airspace layers (MOAs, restricted areas, TFRs, military routes) on and off.

    2 Read the map

    The pin shape says towered, non-towered or military. A green dot means land, fuel and RON are all approved; a yellow dot means see the comments on the list. Click any pin, or search by code, name or FBO at the top, for its sheet: FBO and phone, runway, fuel, FBO hours, sunrise and sunset, approaches, per diem and state lodging tax, and a forecast by the hour.

    3 Check the weather

    The Weather box on the map starts with just radar. Open it to pick a day, slide through the hours in local time, and switch on flight category (MVFR IFR LIFR), ceiling, visibility, icing, precipitation and thunderstorms.

    4 Build a flight plan

    Open Flight plan (the tab on the right edge of the map). Type an arrival field in the box, or tap Add to trip on a field's sheet. Each row is one leg: departure, arrival, takeoff time (in that field's local time, change it any time), flight time, and the landing time in the arrival field's local time and Zulu. Use Details on a leg to reorder it or see the forecast chips.

    5 What the plan tells you

    Take off on a later day than you landed and you are assumed RON: those nights count toward per diem. Warnings show under a leg: night landing (after evening civil twilight or before morning), FBO closed, tight on gas (400+ nm), and legs that overlap. If the forecast at either end is MVFR, IFR or LIFR, a tab opens to a ceiling timeline from your takeoff time.

    6 Print the crew card

    In the flight plan, Print crew card makes a one-page reference: time zones (and whether you gain or lose an hour), every leg in local and Zulu, runways of 4,000 × 75 ft or more, FBO phone numbers, approvals, fuel and AIR Card merchants, per diem, and closure NOTAMs. Copy route gives a route string you can paste into ForeFlight.

    7 Events and weekend recs

    The Events tab maps concerts, festivals, rodeos, sports and more in towns within range for the dates you choose. Weekend Recs picks the best event for each upcoming weekend, with two backups and a weather outlook; click a city name to see it on the Events map.

    8 Share it

    The page address holds everything you set: base, range, layers, your flight plan and its times. Copy it from your browser to send someone the same view. Press ? How to use at the top to bring this screen back.

    Planning reference only. Confirm hours, fuel, PPR and approvals with FLIP, the current Nav Info List and base ops, and check NOTAMs, TFRs and a real weather briefing before you go.
